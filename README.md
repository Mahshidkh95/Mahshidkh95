# 👋 Hi, I’m Mahshid

**Building Science Researcher | Experimental Building Physics | Ventilation | Building Energy | Data-Driven Modelling**

I am a Building Science researcher with a completed Master's degree in Architecture–Building Science from Shahid Beheshti University. My research focuses on the experimental and computational assessment of building performance, with particular interest in ventilation, energy efficiency, thermo-aerodynamic behaviour, and data-driven performance analysis.
My Master's research involved the experimental development and evaluation of a solar-assisted ventilation system under independently controlled thermal and wind conditions. I currently work in research and development, combining laboratory measurements, building-performance simulation, CFD, and regression-based modelling to investigate energy-efficient and climate-responsive building systems.
